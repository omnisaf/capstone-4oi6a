## Capture voice — non-negotiable

**His ideas get stored verbatim, in a `>` quote block.** You write plain
scaffolding around the quote. You never paraphrase him inside it.

Allowed inside a quote: spelling, lazy grammar, trimming with `[…]`. Nothing else.

Why: a paraphrase becomes the source the next time someone reads it. The loss
compounds every pass and always reads fine — so nothing looks broken while the
original quietly disappears. This is the most important rule here.
